PQM Support

PQM is a paid ($2.99) single-player crawler. No account. Progress lives on your phone.

How to play

Name a pebble, pick Brawler / Sling / Sage, rest on the Sunny Sill, buy kit at the stall, and put it on the body. Crawl the Crack, the Deeps, the Rift, then the Summit.

Saves

Saves are on-device. Uninstalling the app removes them. There is no cloud restore.
