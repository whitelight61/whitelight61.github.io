PQM — Privacy Policy

Effective 6 September 2026. PQM (PebbleQuest-Mobile) is a single-player game by Cody Boomer.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not create an account, does not use analytics, does not show ads, and does not sell or share personal data.

What stays on your device

Your save (name, class, kit, progress) is stored locally on the device. Deleting the app deletes the save. We cannot read it.

Internet

The app does not require a network connection to play. Optional Support and Privacy links open in the device browser.

Children

PQM is a fantasy crawler rated for ages 9 and up. We do not knowingly collect data from children because we do not collect data at all.
